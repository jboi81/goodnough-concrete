# Goodnough Concrete LLC — Website

I built this website for Goodnough Concrete LLC, my brother-in-law Drew's concrete company in Simpsonville, SC. Before this, the business only had a Facebook page and a TikTok — this is its first real website.

**Live site:** https://goodnough-concrete.netlify.app

## What the site does

- Showcases the services: driveways, patios & walkways, slabs & foundations, garage floors, stamped & decorative concrete, and removal & replacement
- Photo gallery of real jobs, including a before/after pair
- Customer reviews section
- "Free estimate" request form — submissions are emailed straight to the business through Netlify Forms
- Tap-to-call phone number and links to the business Facebook page and TikTok

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework. Hosted on Netlify.

The photos on the site are real job photos from the business's own posts.
